## 🗣️ Debate & Academic Verbs

Assert (Debate):: To state a fact or belief confidently and forcefully (Afirmar con autoridad).
<!--SR:!2026-11-03,1,230-->
Contend (Debate):: To assert a position strongly in an argument; to struggle with (Sostener / lidiar con).
<!--SR:!2026-11-03,1,230-->
Maintain (Debate):: To state strongly that something is true, especially when doubted (Mantener / sostener).
Posit (Debate):: To assume as a fact; to put forward as a basis of argument (Postular / plantear).
Purport (Debate):: To appear or claim to be or do something, especially falsely (Pretender / aparentar).
Corroborate (Debate):: To confirm or give support to a statement, theory, or finding (Corroborar / respaldar).
Refute (Debate):: To prove a statement, theory, or argument to be wrong or false (Refutar).
Substantiate (Debate):: To provide evidence to support or prove the truth of something (Fundamentar con pruebas).
Undermine (Debate):: To lessen the effectiveness, power, or ability of an argument or person (Socavar).

## 🔗 Advanced Linkers & Connectors

## 🚨 B2 / C1 Speaking Exam High-Impact Idioms

It never crossed my mind that...:: I never considered or realized that... (Nunca se me pasó por la cabeza que...).
Before I realised what was happening...:: Describing a sudden shock or crime incident (Antes de que me diera cuenta de lo que ocurría...).
My mind went completely blank:: Unable to think, remember, or react under stress (Mi mente se quedó en blanco).
To pinpoint the exact time/location:: To find, identify, or describe with great precision (Precisar el momento o lugar exacto).
To fall for a scam:: To be deceived by a con artist (Caer en una estafa).
In the vicinity of:: In the area near or surrounding a place (En las inmediaciones de).

## 💻 Tech & LeetCode Patterns (Quick Recall)

Two Sum optimal strategy:: Use a HashMap/Dictionary storing `target - num` with index in O(n) time.
Detect cycle in a Linked List:: Floyd's Cycle Detection (Tortoise and Hare fast & slow pointers).
Find longest substring without repeating characters:: Sliding Window technique with a Set or Map.
Valid Parentheses check:: Stack (LIFO data structure).
Clean Code - Single Responsibility Principle (SRP):: A class/module should have one, and only one, reason to change.
Clean Code - Dependency Inversion Principle (DIP):: High-level modules should not depend on low-level modules; both should depend on abstractions.
