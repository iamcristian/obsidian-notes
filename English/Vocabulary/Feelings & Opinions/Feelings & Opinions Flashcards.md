### Apprehensive
?
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Español:** Aprehensivo, temeroso, inquieto
- **Definition:** Anxious or fearful that something bad or unpleasant will happen.
- **Example:** *"Junior developers were apprehensive about deploying the major database migration to production."*
- **Collocations:** *apprehensive about, apprehensive of, feel apprehensive*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Describe inquietud anticipatoria ante un evento futuro incierto.
- **Word Family:** apprehension (n), apprehensively (adv)

### Reluctant
?
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Español:** Reticente, reacio, poco dispuesto
- **Definition:** Unwilling and hesitant; disinclined.
- **Example:** *"The principal architect was reluctant to introduce another distributed cache layer without clear benchmarks."*
- **Collocations:** *reluctant to do something, reluctant agreement*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Rige normalmente infinitivo con to (*reluctant to do*).
- **Word Family:** reluctance (n), reluctantly (adv)

### Despondent
?

### Vexed
?
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective [Formal]
- **Español:** Irritado, exasperado; (asunto) espinoso / controvertido
- **Definition:** Annoyed, frustrated, or worried; of a problem: much debated and difficult.
- **Example:** *"Cache invalidation remains one of the most vexed questions in computer science."*
- **Collocations:** *vexed question, vexed issue, feel vexed*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Tiene doble significado: una persona enfadada (*feel vexed*) o un problema espinoso (*a vexed issue*).
- **Word Family:** vex (v), vexation (n)

### Cynical
?
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Español:** Cínico, desconfiado de la naturaleza humana
- **Definition:** Believing that people are motivated purely by self-interest; distrustful of human sincerity or integrity.
- **Example:** *"Years of broken corporate promises had made him deeply cynical about company loyalty."*
- **Collocations:** *cynical view, cynical attitude, deeply cynical*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Más allá del escepticismo: asume que todo el mundo actúa por egoísmo oculto.
- **Word Family:** cynic (n), cynicism (n), cynically (adv)

### Laud

### Censure
?
- **Part of Speech:** Verb / Noun [Formal]
- **Español:** Censurar (reprender oficialmente) / Voto de censura
- **Definition:** Express severe disapproval of someone or something, especially in a formal statement.
- **Example:** *"The regulatory board severely censured the tech conglomerate for multiple consumer privacy breaches."*
- **Collocations:** *vote of censure, severe censure, face censure*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** ¡Cuidado con falsos amigos! En inglés, *censure* significa reprensión oficial por mala conducta; tapar información es *censor*.
- **Word Family:** censorious (adj)

?

- **Español:** Eufórico / En estado de júbilo exultante
- **Definition:** Characterized by or feeling intense excitement, elation, and happiness.

- **Part of Speech:** Adjective [Literary / Formal]
- **Español:** Desamparado, desolado, desesperanzado
- **Definition:** Pitifully sad, abandoned, and lonely; unlikely to succeed or be fulfilled.
- **Example:** *"After budget cuts halted construction, the half-built cultural centre stood as a forlorn monument to stalled ambition."*
- **Collocations:** *forlorn hope, look forlorn, forlorn figure*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Tiene dos usos clave en C1/C2: emocional (triste y abandonado) y evaluativo en colocación fija *a forlorn hope* (esperanza casi vana).
- **Word Family:** forlornly (adv), forlornness (n)

### Confirmation bias
?
- **Part of Speech:** Noun [Uncountable, Psychology]
- **Español:** Sesgo de confirmación

### Hindsight bias
?
- **Part of Speech:** Noun [Uncountable, Psychology]
- **Español:** Sesgo retrospectivo ('yo ya lo sabía')
- **Definition:** The inclination, after an event has occurred, to perceive the outcome as having been predictable all along.

### Sunk-cost fallacy
?
- **Part of Speech:** Noun [Singular, Economics / Psychology]
- **Español:** Falacia del costo hundido
- **Definition:** The irrational tendency to continue investing time or money in a failing endeavor simply because resources have already been spent.

- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Recurso argumentativo brillante para Part 1 Essays o informes de gestión cuando se justifica cancelar un proyecto inviable.

### Cognitive dissonance

- **Español:** Disonancia cognitiva
- **Definition:** The state of mental discomfort arising from holding two or more conflicting beliefs, values, or attitudes simultaneously.
