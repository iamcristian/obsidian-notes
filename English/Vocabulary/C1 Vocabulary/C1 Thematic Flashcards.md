### Autonomous
?
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Español:** Autónomo, independiente
- **Definition:** Acting independently or having the freedom to govern itself.
- **Example:** *"Cross-functional engineering squads operate as autonomous units capable of deploying without bureaucracy."*
- **Collocations:** *autonomous team, autonomous system, fully autonomous*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Describe independencia operativa y capacidad de toma de decisiones sin tutela externa.
- **Word Family:** autonomy (n), autonomously (adv)

---

### Redundancy
?
- **Part of Speech:** Noun [Uncountable/Countable]
- **Español:** Redundancia (en sistemas); despido laboral (UK)
- **Definition:** The inclusion of extra components functioning in case of failure; in British English: state of being laid off.
- **Example:** *"Geographic redundancy ensures that if an entire cloud region fails, traffic automatically reroutes to another continent."*
- **Collocations:** *built-in redundancy, network redundancy, make redundant*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** ¡Doble sentido! En ingeniería es positivo (fiabilidad); en el mundo laboral británico significa despido por reestructuración (*made redundant*).
- **Word Family:** redundant (adj)

### Scalability
?
- **Part of Speech:** Noun [Uncountable]
- **Español:** Escalabilidad
- **Definition:** The capacity to be changed in size or scale, especially the ability of a system to handle growing amounts of work.
- **Example:** *"Horizontal scalability allows the platform to absorb Black Friday traffic surges by spawning container replicas."*
- **Collocations:** *horizontal scalability, vertical scalability, massive scalability*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Propiedad arquitectónica central en computación en la nube.
- **Word Family:** scale (v/n), scalable (adj)

### Inherent
?
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Español:** Inherente, intrínseco
- **Definition:** Existing in something as a permanent, essential, or characteristic attribute.
- **Example:** *"Network latency and partial network partitions are inherent challenges of distributed computing."*
- **Collocations:** *inherent risk, inherent flaw, inherent characteristic*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Rige normalmente *in* o *to* (*inherent in the design*).
- **Word Family:** inherently (adv)

### Mitigate
?
- **Part of Speech:** Verb [Transitive, Formal]
- **Español:** Mitigar, atenuar, reducir la gravedad de
- **Definition:** Make something bad less severe, serious, or painful.
- **Example:** *"Implementing automated rate limiting is essential to mitigate the impact of distributed denial-of-service attacks."*
- **Collocations:** *mitigate risks, mitigate damage, mitigate the effects*
- **Nuance & Exam Tip:** Se combina casi obligatoriamente con *risks, effects, damage*.
- **Word Family:** mitigation (n), unmitigated (adj)

?
