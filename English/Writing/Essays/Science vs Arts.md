# Science vs Arts

## Question
Some people think that schools should spend more money on art and music lessons than on science subjects. Do you agree?

**Write about:**

- creativity
- future jobs
- **one idea of your own**

**New phrases:**
- This is because
- For this reason
- One advantage of... is that...

---

Some people argue that schools should spend more money on art and music lessons than on science subjects. Nowadays, art and music are an important part of our lives, but science plays a crucial role in the development of society.

Art and music lessons help students express their ideas. For this reason, students become more creative and develop their imagination, which helps students develop their own personality as well as their critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.

On the other hand, science subjects are responsible for many of today's major breakthroughs. Fields such as technology, medicine, biology, mathematics, physics, and chemistry play an important role in society's development. Nevertheless, art and music lessons are part of the intellectual development which is required for whatever field of study.

In conclusion, I believe that schools should not spend more money on art and music lessons than on science subjects. In order to prepare students for the future, society should prioritize the students who want to make a contribution to the world. Art and music are part of leisure activities  that are not critical like science.
