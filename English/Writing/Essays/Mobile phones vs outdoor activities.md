# Mobile phones vs outdoor activities

## Question

Some people believe that young people should spend less time using mobile phones and more time doing outdoor activities. Do you agree?

- social life
- **one idea of your own**

**Restrictions:**

The issue of using mobile phones instead of spending more time on outdoor activities has sparked intense discussion in recent years. Notwithstanding the benefits of technology, a considerable number of people believe that outdoor activities are better than using mobile phones for young people.

A compelling reason to do outdoor activities is social life. Human being are social by nature, so it is crucial to develop communication skills during childhood and adolescence. For instance, If this issue is not taken into consideration, a person could have emotional problems and social isolation. As a result, that person will not be able to fit in society properly.

Conversely, a person who knows how to use a mobile phone can have a balanced life. Interacting in the real world with face-to-face interactions and also make the most of mobile phones. For example, a young person can do some outdoor activities such as playing a sport or taking an extracurricular course. Consequently, young people can use their mobile phones and do outdoor activities to avoid diseases, which is one of the main concerns of people.

In my view, mobile phones are an essential part of young people to date. People can use them to learn almost everything All the information is available online and recently, someone can use Artificial Intelligence to ask and learn easily. The advantages outweigh the disadvantages.

In conclusion, taking everything into account, I believe that using such a valuable tool is a benefit to any person. Even though the advantages, young people need self-awareness. Having said that if a person knows how to use it and takes responsibility they could enjoy the best of both worlds.
