# Should Public Transport Be Free?

## Question
Some people think that public transport should be free for everyone. Do you agree?

Some people argue that public transport should be free for everyone. Public transport helps people travel from one place to another with an affordable fare, which helps cover the driver's salary and the vehicle maintenance. Although free public transport offers several advantages, it also presents some challenges, which should be carefully considered.

On the one hand, free public transport could play an important role in low-income people. A person needs to commute to work at least 2 times per day, the same is true for students. Saving up a bit of money every day might be helpful for low-income population, this could encourage people to continue studying or working.

However, making public transport free would be expensive for governments. As a result, they might have less money to invest in other important areas such as healthcare, education or environmental protection.

Taking everything into account, I believe that governments should prioritize more environmentally friendly alternatives like electric cars or bicycles to forestall environmental pollution in the future.
