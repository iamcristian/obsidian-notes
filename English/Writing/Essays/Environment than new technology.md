# Environment than new technology

## Question

Some people think that governments should invest more money in protecting the environment than in developing new technology. Do you agree?

**Write about:**

- environmental protection
- technological development
- **one idea of your own**

Nowadays, many people are concerned to some extent about the environment. This is mainly because modern technology contributes to pollution. A growing number of people believe that governments should invest more money in protecting the environment than in developing new technology. Technology has led to remarkable breakthroughs such as Artificial Intelligence, Internet, smartphones, and others. The only drawback is the huge pollution that it creates.

On the one hand, despite the benefits of technology, preserving natural resources and tackling climate change are a global concern. Every day, smog can be seen in many cities, that causes deseases, therefore, air quality and living conditions are becoming inadequate. Scientists claim that one day certain places will become uninhabitable to live due to pollution. As a result, unknown deseases will break out.

On the other hand, technology drives innovation for the sake of the world. As a matter of fact, technology helps develop new medicines and disease treatments. For instance, Scientist discover treatments for COVID-19 faster than before thanks to technology a few years ago. Therefore, numerous people's lives have been saved due to quick actions.

It is my firm conviction that technology is a crucial tool for the world. Even though I feel compelled to complain about the pollution. Protecting the environment is crucial, so I believe that governments should establish new laws to prevent that big companies and factories do not increase pollution and encourage them to adopt greener practices for the sake of world.
