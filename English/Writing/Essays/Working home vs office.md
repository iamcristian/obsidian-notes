# Working home vs office

## Question

Some people think that working from home is better than working in an office. Do you agree?

**Write about:**

- productivity
- work-life balance
- **one idea of your own**

**New phrases:**
- This is mainly because...
- As a result,...
- For instance...

**Example structure a paragraph:**
- Working from home saves time. This is mainly because people do not need to conmute. For instance, someone who usually spends two hours travelling every day could use that time to rest or study. As a result, they may feel less stressed and become more productive.

---

The issue of working from home instead of an office has sparked intense discussion in recent years. There are those who believe that it is essential to have face-to-face interactions, despite the fact that many white-collar workers now work remotely.

One key advantage is time management. One reason is that people save commuting time by working remotely. As a result, workers increase productivity and reduce stress. For instance, software engineers have been working remotely in big tech companies such as Google, Facebook, and Amazon for a long time. As a result, these companies are more competitive because of hiring international talent in any country. 

On the other hand, working in an office is still crucial for certain jobs like mechanics, doctor, dentist, electricians, and so on. In fact, people have different behaviors and traits that it is impossible to claim working from home is the best option. Some people are not able to avoid distractions and concentrate at home. Therefore, it depends upon the goals and level of interaction people need most.

As far as I am aware, as a systems engineer, remote jobs are the best opportunity to work internationally without leaving your country. On top of that, people can improve work-life balance and earn higher salaries. Conversely, it is not possible for all the jobs. If a person has the option to try these kinds of jobs, it would be an important step in his life.

In conclusion, taking all factors into account, the best option is to give it a try. Perhaps it does not fit a person's personality or kind of job, but if it is not the case, it is an captivating world to explore.
